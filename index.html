Speed, Precision, and Absolute Privacy.

Every tool in Voca is built natively to eliminate the friction of personal financial bookkeeping.

Instant Vision OCR Scanner

Point your camera at any paper receipt. Apple Vision machine learning extracts the total, tax, and merchant in milliseconds without cloud delay.

Hands-Free Siri Shortcuts

Speak naturally while driving or walking. Siri Shortcuts and AppIntents parse amounts and categories directly into your ledger without opening the app.

Comprehensive Audit Reports

Analyze spending patterns across customizable date ranges with Swift Charts. Export itemized RFC-4180 CSV files or formatted PDF audit summaries.

Live Activities & Dynamic Island

Keep your daily spending pace visible at a glance. Real-time Live Activities show budget status directly on your iPhone Lock Screen.

100% Private iCloud Sync

Your financial transactions are never stored on third-party servers. All data synchronizes across your devices using encrypted Apple CloudKit storage.
