Troubleshooting Guides

Receipt Scan Not Recognizing Text?

1. Check that the receipt is well-lit and not crumpled.

2. Keep the camera steady directly above the receipt.

3. If a thermal paper receipt is very faded, use the manual edit sheet to fine-tune the amount or merchant name.

iCloud Sync Not Updating Across Devices?

1. Ensure both devices are signed in with the same Apple ID.

2. Confirm that iCloud Drive and Voca are toggled ON in iOS Settings > [Your Apple ID] > iCloud.

3. Verify that your iPhone is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular data and is not in Low Data Mode.
